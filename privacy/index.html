Dört Köşe — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026 · Türkçe

Dört Köşe is a mobile app that provides football statistics and predictions. The app does not take bets, does not accept money, and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Data we collect

  • Anonymous session identifier: the app works without an account. A random identifier, not linked to your real-world identity, is created so your preferences can be stored. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or payment details.
  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, an Expo push token is stored so we can deliver them to your device. You can turn this off at any time from your phone settings or inside the app.
  • Notification preferences and saved predictions: the choices you make in the app are stored against your anonymous identifier.
  • What you write: messages you post in the community chat or in match comments are stored with their timestamp and are visible to other users. They appear under a nickname generated for you, never under your real name.
  • Advertising identifier: used as described under "Advertising measurement" below.

Advertising measurement

The app includes Meta's (Facebook) measurement SDK, which uses your device's advertising identifier. We use it for one purpose only: to measure whether our own ads are working, so we do not show you ads you do not need.

On iPhone the choice is yours. Apple's tracking permission prompt appears the first time you open the app. If you decline, your advertising identifier is not used and measurement runs disabled. You can change this later in your phone settings.

On Android you can reset the advertising identifier or turn off personalisation from your device's advertising settings.

No advertising is shown inside the app. This SDK is for measurement only.

What we do not collect

We do not access your location, contacts, photos, or microphone. Your data is never sold.

Service providers

  • Supabase: database and server infrastructure. The data listed above is hosted there.
  • Expo: delivery of push notifications and app updates. Only the push token is processed.
  • Meta (Facebook): advertising measurement. The advertising identifier and basic usage events such as app opens are processed. On iPhone this happens only if you granted permission.

What you write, and moderation

The community chat and match comments are public. Objectionable content (profanity, insults, phone numbers, account details, links pointing off the app, and guaranteed win claims) is blocked automatically. Reported content is reviewed and hidden where necessary; we respond to reports within 24 hours. If you block a user, you will no longer see anything they write.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only for as long as the app needs it to work. Deleting the app removes local data from your device; you can request deletion of your anonymous server-side record and everything you wrote using the contact address below.
