Dört Köşe — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Türkçe

Dört Köşe is a mobile app that provides football statistics and predictions. The app does not take bets, does not accept money, and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Data controller: Dört Köşe Şans Oyunları Ltd. Şti. (tax no. 3131543834), 17 Eylül Mah. 1022 Sk. No: 1C, Bandırma / Balıkesir, Turkey. The KVKK (Turkish data protection law) notice for membership data is inside the app: Profil › Aydınlatma metni.

Data we collect

  • Anonymous session identifier: the app works without an account. A random identifier, not linked to your real-world identity, is created so your preferences can be stored. We do not ask for your name or address.
  • Membership: email address and mobile number (optional): if you sign up you can get back into your account on another phone or after signing out. There is no password: a 6-digit code is emailed to you to sign up and to sign in. The mobile number is not verified and we never send SMS or marketing messages to it; it is used to match your dealer membership and to reach you if needed. Without membership the app keeps working with an anonymous session.
  • Dealer application screenshot (optional): if you apply for Pro through your dealer membership, the screenshot you pick is kept only to review the application and is deleted once a decision is made.
  • Subscription status: payment is taken by the App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your card details. The store tells us only which product the subscription is for and how long it is valid, which we keep in order to unlock your access. This flow runs through RevenueCat Inc.
  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, an Expo push token is stored so we can deliver them to your device. You can turn this off at any time from your phone settings or inside the app.
  • Notification preferences and saved predictions: the choices you make in the app are stored against your anonymous identifier.
  • What you write: messages you post in the community chat or in match comments are stored with their timestamp and are visible to other users. They appear under a nickname generated for you, never under your real name.
  • Advertising identifier: used as described under "Advertising measurement" below.

Advertising measurement

The app includes Meta's (Facebook) measurement SDK. We use it for one purpose only: to measure whether our own ads are working, so we do not show you ads you do not need. Usage events such as app opens, viewing the Pro page, starting a purchase and starting a subscription are sent to Meta. For the subscription event we send only the period (weekly or monthly); Meta's SDK may also log the store purchase, including its amount, automatically. Card and payment details are never sent to Meta.

On iPhone the choice is yours. Apple's tracking permission prompt appears the first time you open the app. If you decline, your advertising identifier is not used and we do not track you across other companies' apps and websites; usage events are still sent, without the advertising identifier, for aggregated measurement only. You can change this later in your phone settings.

On Android you can reset the advertising identifier or turn off personalisation from your device's advertising settings.

No advertising is shown inside the app. This SDK is for measurement only.

What we do not collect

We do not access your location, contacts, or microphone. We do not access your photos; only the single image you pick for a dealer application is sent. We never see, process, or store your card or payment details — payment happens entirely on the App Store or Google Play. Your data is never sold.

Service providers

  • Supabase: database and server infrastructure. The data listed above is hosted there.
  • Expo: delivery of push notifications and app updates. Only the push token is processed.
  • Resend: delivery of sign-in and sign-up code emails. Only your email address and the code are processed.
  • RevenueCat: subscription management. Processes the subscription information returned by the store (product and expiry date) together with your in-app user identifier. No card details are involved.
  • Meta (Facebook): advertising measurement. Usage events (app opens, the Pro page, purchase and subscription starts) and, if you allowed it, the advertising identifier are processed. On iPhone the advertising identifier is not sent unless you granted permission.

What you write, and moderation

The community chat and match comments are public. Objectionable content (profanity, insults, phone numbers, account details, links pointing off the app, and guaranteed win claims) is blocked automatically. Reported content is reviewed and hidden where necessary; we respond to reports within 24 hours. If you block a user, you will no longer see anything they write.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only for as long as the app needs it to work. Deleting the app removes local data from your device. Your email address and mobile number are kept while your account exists. Our infrastructure providers' servers are outside Turkey, so data is processed abroad; your explicit consent is asked for when you sign up. You can delete your account and all of your server-side data from inside the app: Profile › My account › Delete account. Steps and what is deleted (in Turkish): Hesap ve veri silme. Deletion cannot be undone, and can also be requested using the contact address below. Deleting your account does not cancel your store subscription; you need to cancel that from your App Store or Google Play account.
