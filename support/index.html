Frequently asked

Does the app take bets?
No. Dört Köşe does not take bets, does not accept money and does not place coupons. It provides statistics and predictions only, and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Are the predictions guaranteed?
No. We never claim a prediction will win. That is exactly why every rule's measured hit rate is shown in the app; rules without enough settled results get no percentage at all, and experimental ones are labelled as experimental.

How do I turn notifications off?
In the app's Profile section you can disable all notifications, keep only live goal alerts, or enable quiet hours at night. You can also disable them from your phone settings.

Account and data
The app works without an account; we do not ask for your name, email or payment details. See the privacy policy for details. To have your records deleted, email the address above.

A score or odds value looks wrong
Match data comes from external providers and can occasionally lag. Send us the match name and kick-off time and we will check it.
